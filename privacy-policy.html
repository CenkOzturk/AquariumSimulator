Privacy Policy for My Kukuro Aquarium

Effective Date: October 14, 2026

KukuroDev ("we", "us", or "our") operates My Kukuro Aquarium (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our Game.

1. Information We Collect

To provide online features, cloud saving, and account authentication, we collect the following limited information:

  • Account & Profile Data: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and unique user ID (UID) provided by Google Authentication.
  • Game Save Data: Your game progress (coins, inventory items, aquarium setups, and preferences) is saved to Firebase Cloud Firestore.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity via Google Sign-In.
  • To synchronize and restore your game save files across devices or app reinstalls.
  • To ensure fair play and prevent game data loss.

3. Third-Party Services

We use reputable third-party services provided by Google to power our game infrastructure:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages user login securely.
  • Firebase Firestore: Stores encrypted game saves and user progress in the cloud.

These services process data under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or marketing companies.

4. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data. All communication between the Game and Firebase services is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

5. Account & Data Deletion Rights

You have full control over your personal data. If you wish to delete your account, game save data, or personal information associated with My Kukuro Aquarium:

  • Send an email request to kukurodev@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  • Please send the request from the email address linked to your Google Sign-In account.

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently erase your user profile and all associated save data from our Firebase servers within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

My Kukuro Aquarium does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated Effective Date.
