My Kukuro Aquarium currently does not collect, transmit, or share personal information from users.

The Game does not currently require users to create an account or provide personal information such as:

My Kukuro Aquarium stores game progress and game-related data locally on the user's device.

3. Data Sharing

We do not currently sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

My Kukuro Aquarium currently does not use third-party services that collect or process personal information.

We take reasonable measures to protect locally stored game data.

However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because game data is currently stored locally on the user's device, users can remove this data by deleting the Game and its associated application data from their device.
